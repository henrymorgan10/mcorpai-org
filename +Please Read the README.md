I currently work as an independent developer and AI systems architect. My research takes a different approach from conventional AI systems that depend heavily on cloud infrastructure and large data centers. In particular, I am working on offline AI architectures that can operate independently where internet access or server infrastructure is limited or unavailable, while reducing by design the risk of personal or sensitive data leaving the local device or environment.

The small independent security and AI research institute I hope to establish would focus on several areas:

- **Offline AI and Edge AI Architecture** — AI systems designed to make decisions and operate locally without relying on cloud or server connectivity.

- **AI Security and Privacy-Preserving Technology** — secure architectures that minimize external communications, unnecessary data collection, and dependence on third-party services.

- **Zero-Dependency Systems** — self-contained systems that reduce reliance on external libraries, CDNs, and APIs, helping limit exposure to supply-chain attacks, service outages, and external infrastructure failures.

- **Explainable and Verifiable AI** — transparent systems in which people can understand how decisions are reached, recognize the system's limitations, and retain final authority over important decisions.

- **Disaster Response, Safety, and Local Early-Warning Systems** — affordable detection and warning technologies designed for disaster zones, remote areas, and other environments where communications networks or data-center infrastructure may not be available.

- **Distributed Local Communication Technologies** — device-to-device communication and emergency networks using technologies such as BLE mesh networking, acoustic communication, and ultrasonic communication without depending on the internet.

- **Critical Infrastructure and Closed-Network Security** — independent AI systems for industrial facilities, manufacturing environments, healthcare, public safety, and other settings where external connectivity may be restricted or undesirable.

- **Low-Power, Low-Cost AI and ESG-Oriented Technology** — practical AI that can operate on older smartphones, inexpensive sensors, and low-power hardware, with the aim of making useful technology more accessible and sustainable.

Some of this work has already developed into more specific technical research, including passive Bluetooth scanning and RSSI-based positioning for locating missing persons, BLE mesh networks for environments without internet access, SOS relay systems for emergencies, and distributed sensing systems for disaster situations.

For this reason, I believe technology designed specifically to improve their safety, independence, and quality of life can carry meaningful social value.

The AI Safety Necklace is intended to explore how accessible, privacy-conscious technology can help protect vulnerable children while supporting their everyday independence. If developed responsibly and validated in cooperation with appropriate specialists and institutions, I believe this project has the potential to become an important part of the research institute’s ESG mission and to demonstrate how AI research can create direct and measurable social impact.

I hope the institute will ultimately be judged not only by the technology it develops, but also by **who that technology is able to protect and support**.
